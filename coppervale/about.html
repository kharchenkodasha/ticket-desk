About Coppervale

A North Carolina company, rooted in the land we build on.

Coppervale Land was founded to do land acquisition and development the right way: patient, transparent, and grounded in local knowledge.

Our story

Named for the color of Carolina clay.

The copper-red soil of the Piedmont is where much of North Carolina's growth is happening. Coppervale was founded by people who grew up here and want that growth done well.

We buy land directly from owners, many of whom have held it for generations, and carry each property through planning, entitlement and development.

We are not a high-volume land flipper. We take on a limited number of properties each year, and we stay involved from the first call to the last finished lot.

What guides us

Three commitments.

Respect the ground

We plan around terrain, water and trees instead of flattening them, because the land gives a place its value.

Say it plainly

Written offers, plain-language contracts and honest answers, including when a property is not a fit.

Stay local

We work in counties we know, with local engineers, surveyors, attorneys and builders.

Who we work with

Long-term relationships across the state.

Our best projects come from people who know us: landowners who refer neighbors, attorneys who trust our closings, and builders who return for the next phase.

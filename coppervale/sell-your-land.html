Seller questions

Common questions.

How do you decide on an offer price?

We look at comparable land sales, zoning, utilities, access, soils and floodplain, and what the site can realistically support. We explain how we reached the number.

Do I need a survey or perc test first?

No. We handle surveys, soil evaluations and other studies during due diligence, at our cost.

What if several family members own the land?

That is common with inherited and heirs' property. We work with every owner and with the closing attorney to make sure the title is clear.

Can I keep using the land until closing?

Yes. Farming, hunting leases and other current uses can usually continue until the day we close.
